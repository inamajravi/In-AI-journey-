Apply for Tech Lead / Solution Architect / AI Engineering Lead roles

Level 5 · Topic 5 of 5

Why it matters

Everything above matters most when it shows up clearly in your story, your CV and your interviews.

Core concepts

  • Narrative: senior full-stack engineer who architects and reviews AI-assisted systems, with a production sanctions platform, evals, security awareness and certifications as evidence.
  • CV: lead with outcomes and numbers (e.g. screening from minutes to milliseconds), the architect/reviewer role, and credentials.
  • Interview prep: be ready to whiteboard an AI system end to end, justify RAG vs agent vs workflow, discuss evals, cost, security and failure handling.
  • Case study: prepare a 10-minute walkthrough of the sanctions platform with architecture, tradeoffs, ADRs and lessons.
  • Pipeline: target a weekly number of tailored applications and referrals; track them in a simple sheet.

Hands-on exercise

Write your 10-minute case-study script. Do two mock interviews with a peer or Claude: one system-design, one behavioural. Revise based on where you hesitated.

Self-check

  1. Can you explain every decision in your platform and what you'd change?
  2. What is your one-sentence positioning?
  3. How many tailored applications will you send each week?
